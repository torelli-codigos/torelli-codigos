Full Stack Developer with 4+ years of experience, primarily focused on backend development using Laravel, PHP, and modern JavaScript technologies.

Since July 2022, I've contributed to 8+ team-based projects, building and maintaining production applications across different domains and levels of complexity.

Beyond development, I actively contribute to technical decisions, feature planning, requirements definition, and team collaboration. I'm passionate about building scalable, maintainable solutions that solve real business problems.

Always open to interesting projects, technical discussions, knowledge sharing, and new connections. Feel free to reach out!
